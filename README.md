# BLACKJACK (21) – RULES

1. **Objective:** Get a hand total closer to 21 than the dealer without exceeding 21.

2. **Card Values:**
   - Cards 2–10 have their face value.
   - Jack, Queen and King = 10 points.
   - Ace = 1 or 11 points, whichever is more beneficial.

3. **Starting the Game:**
   - Each player receives two cards.
   - The dealer receives two cards, usually one face up and one face down.
   - Players take turns making decisions.

4. **Player Actions:**
   - **Hit:** Take another card.
   - **Stand:** Keep your current total and end your turn.
   - **Double Down:** Double your wager, take exactly one more card, then stand.
   - **Split:** Separate two cards of equal value into two hands by placing an additional wager.
   - **Surrender:** If allowed, forfeit half your wager and end your hand.

5. **Blackjack:**
   - An Ace and a 10-value card in the first two cards make Blackjack (21).
   - Blackjack usually beats a regular hand totaling 21.
   - If both player and dealer have Blackjack, it is a push.

6. **Bust:**
   - A total above 21 is a bust.
   - A busted player automatically loses.

7. **Dealer Rules:**
   - Dealer must hit on 16 or below.
   - Dealer must stand on hard 17 or above.
   - Some tables require hitting on soft 17.

8. **Soft and Hard Hands:**
   - Soft hand: Contains an Ace counted as 11.
   - Hard hand: Has no Ace counted as 11.

9. **Winning:**
   - Higher valid total than the dealer wins.
   - If the dealer busts, remaining players win.
   - If both have equal totals, it is a push (tie).
   - If the player busts, they lose.

10. **Insurance:**
    - Offered when the dealer shows an Ace.
    - Usually costs half the original wager.
    - Pays 2:1 if the dealer has Blackjack.

11. **Payouts:**
    - Normal win: 1:1.
    - Blackjack: Usually 3:2 or 6:5.
    - Push: Original wager returned.
    - Loss: Wager forfeited.

12. **End of Round:**
    - Players finish their turns.
    - Dealer reveals the hidden card.
    - Dealer completes their hand.
    - Results are calculated and the next round begins.

**Note:** Rules for splitting, doubling, surrendering and dealer actions may vary by table.
